# ArbiX User Onboarding Plan for CFD Trading

This document outlines the essential information required to onboard a user for CFD (Contracts for Difference) trading on the ArbiX platform, adhering to regulatory compliance (KYC/AML) and risk management best practices.

## 1. Personal Identification Information (KYC)
-   **Full Legal Name**: As it appears on official government-issued documents.
-   **Date of Birth**: To verify legal age requirements.
-   **Nationality**: For regulatory reporting and compliance with international laws.
-   **Contact Information**: Valid email address and phone number for communication and verification.

## 2. Residential Information
-   **Full Residential Address**: Including street, city, postal code, and country.
-   **Proof of Address**: A recent utility bill, bank statement, or government-issued document (typically dated within the last 3-6 months) to verify residency.

## 3. Identity Verification Documents (Proof of Identity)
-   **Government-Issued Photo ID**: A clear copy or scan of a valid passport, national ID card, or driver's license. This is crucial for identity verification.

## 4. Financial Information
-   **Employment Status and Occupation**: To understand the user's primary source of income.
-   **Annual Income**: To assess financial capacity and suitability for leveraged products.
-   **Source of Funds/Wealth**: Documentation or declaration explaining how the funds used for trading were acquired (e.g., salary, savings, inheritance). This is critical for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance.
-   **Net Worth (or Liquid Net Worth)**: An assessment of the user's total financial assets to evaluate their overall financial health and ability to withstand potential losses.
-   **Bank Account Details**: Information for linking bank accounts for deposits and withdrawals, including bank name, account number, and SWIFT/IBAN (if applicable).

## 5. Trading Experience and Knowledge (Suitability Assessment)
-   **Trading Experience**: Questions about the user's prior experience in financial markets, including the number of years trading and specific instruments traded (e.g., Forex, Stocks, Cryptocurrencies, CFDs).
-   **Knowledge Assessment**: A questionnaire designed to gauge the user's understanding of CFD trading, leverage, margin calls, stop-loss orders, and the inherent risks involved.
-   **Risk Tolerance**: An assessment to determine the user's willingness and capacity to take on financial risks.

## 6. Account and Security Information
-   **Desired Account Type**: Choice of account type (e.g., Standard, Professional, Demo) based on their trading needs and experience.
-   **Trading Currency**: Preferred currency for the trading account (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP).
-   **Password/Security Questions**: For secure account access and recovery procedures.
-   **Tax Identification Number (TIN)**: Or equivalent (e.g., Social Security Number, National Insurance Number) for tax reporting purposes in their jurisdiction.

## 7. Regulatory Disclosures and Agreements
-   **Client Agreement/Terms and Conditions**: Users must electronically agree to the platform's terms of service.
-   **Risk Disclosure Statement**: A clear and concise statement outlining the significant risks associated with CFD trading, which the user must acknowledge.
-   **Privacy Policy**: Information on how user data is collected, stored, and processed.
-   **AML/KYC Policy**: Acknowledgment of the platform's compliance with Anti-Money Laundering and Know Your Customer regulations.

This plan ensures that all necessary information is collected for regulatory compliance, risk management, and to provide a secure and suitable trading environment for ArbiX users.
